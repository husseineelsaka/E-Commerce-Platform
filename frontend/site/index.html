    How it works

    What happens behind each button on this page.

    1. Sign in asks Keycloak for a token. The Gateway checks it, deletes any X-User headers you send, adds your real identity, and calls the services with its own service token.
    2. Order checks price (product) and stock (inventory) synchronously, saves the order as PENDING with an outbox event in one transaction, and answers 201 at once.
    3. The Saga continues over Kafka: inventory reserves, payment charges once, order becomes CONFIRMED and notification tells you. If payment fails, stock is released and the order is CANCELLED.
    4. Reviews are stored by review-service; product-service updates the average from a ReviewSubmitted event, so the rating appears a moment later.
